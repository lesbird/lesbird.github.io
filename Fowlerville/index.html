What's special

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 1900 farmhouse offering over 2,000 sq. ft. of living space, plus a full unfinished basement, situated on 2.3 scenic acres. Enjoy peaceful country living with a tree farm across the road and expansive farmland directly behind the property, providing beautiful views and added privacy.

This move-in-ready home features 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, spacious living areas, abundant storage, and a cozy gas fireplace. The kitchen was extensively remodeled in 2024 (excluding the flooring) and includes newer black stainless refrigerator and dishwasher (2024), natural gas range, microwave, washer, dryer, and hot tub—all included with the sale. The primary bathroom was remodeled in 2024, the half bath was updated in 2020, and many additional rooms have been freshly painted.

Numerous major updates provide peace of mind, including a brand-new central A/C (2026), new water softener (2025), generator hookup (2025), updated attic insulation, updated plumbing and electrical, and newer upstairs windows (2018). The well has been recently inspected, the septic was pumped in 2025, and the dry basement has no known history of leaks or flooding.

Outside, you'll find a patio, bonfire pit, and two versatile outbuildings. The impressive two-story barn offers exceptional storage and workspace, while the second two-story barn was re-sided in 2020 and features a custom-built bar and entertainment area with its own wall-mounted A/C unit—perfect for gatherings, hobbies, or a home workshop.

If you've been looking for a property that combines historic farmhouse charm with modern updates, functional outbuildings, and a picturesque country setting, this one is ready to welcome you home.
